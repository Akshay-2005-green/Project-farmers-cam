About FarmersCam

FarmersCam is an Artificial Intelligence based web application developed to help farmers identify crop diseases quickly and accurately. Using Deep Learning and TensorFlow, the system analyzes images of crop leaves and predicts diseases in Tomato, Potato, and Pepper plants.

Features

Disease Detection

Upload crop leaf images and detect diseases instantly.

History

View all your previous predictions.

Profile

Manage your personal account information.

AI Powered

Built using TensorFlow and MobileNetV2.

How FarmersCam Works

1. Upload Image

Select a crop leaf image from your gallery or camera.

2. AI Analysis

The deep learning model analyzes the uploaded image.

3. Prediction

Detects the disease with confidence score.

4. Save Result

Prediction is stored in your history.

Tips for Better Detection

  • 🌿 Capture images in natural daylight.
  • 📷 Keep only one leaf in the frame.
  • 🔍 Ensure the image is clear and focused.
  • 🍃 Place the leaf on a plain background.
  • 🤖 AI works best with healthy lighting conditions.
  • ✅ Supported crops: Tomato, Potato and Pepper.
